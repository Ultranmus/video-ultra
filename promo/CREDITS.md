# Promo video sources

The promo video (`apps/playground/public/home/promo.mp4`) is made with `promo/record.ts` from
these files. They are downloaded into `promo/clips/` (not committed) by `promo/fetch.sh`.

All footage and music is from [Mixkit](https://mixkit.co/license/): the Stock Video Free License
and the Stock Music Free License (free for commercial use, no credit required). Credited here
anyway.
